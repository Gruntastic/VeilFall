Current build ID: VF-G3-20261003-A. UE 5.8.3 CL 58210709. Mouse sensitivity is 0.36 degrees/count: exactly 50% above 0.24. The user authorized progression after reviewing G2. G3 human route/combat/co-op acceptance is pending.

## Play
From G0/VeilFallG0, run `./Scripts/G3.ps1 Host` for a solo listen server, then `./Scripts/G3.ps1 Join` for another local process. LAN clients use the console command `open <host-IP>`. Initial support is LAN/direct IP; internet matchmaking is not implemented.

WASD, mouse, Space, E interact; left mouse fire, right mouse aim, R reload; 1 primary, 2 shotgun, 3 pistol; G medical kit, H ammunition kit; Enter respawn after death. HQ benches swap carbine/marksman, cycle armor, restock and explicitly down SELF for training. HQ supply bank stores field items. Death retains session equipment and drops unbanked field items into one uniquely identified recovery pack. Aim at a pack and press E to claim its complete contents; field capacity is sixteen items.

Follow the current objective. Three Continuity guards protect the relay; three protect the convoy. Clearing terminals now requires that group's guards to be dead. Rifleman seeks flanking cover, support holds nearer home cover and fires bursts, marksman prefers range. Sight must be established through AI Perception and line of sight. Squad memory expires after eight seconds. Enemies periodically peek above cover. This is prototype tactics with primitive bodies, not final animation or tuning.

The host owns the durable campaign. Schema 2 adds consumed pickup IDs, killed enemy IDs, host field inventory, supply stash and recovery packs. A real schema 1 fixture tests migration. An older G1/G2 executable cannot understand schema 2; keep those packages on separate save roots. Do not run multiple hosts on one campaign save. Guests have session inventory only; disconnect converts carried field supplies to a recoverable pack after the server recognizes logout. Guest identities and host migration are outside scope. Host equipment choice is session state, not yet saved across restart.
