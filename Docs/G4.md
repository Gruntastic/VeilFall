# G4 candidate — benchmarks, sprint and cover

Candidate: VF-G4-20261005-E. Engine: UE 5.8.3 CL58210709. G3 package/map/saves are preserved. G4 uses L_DeadSignal_G4 and Saved/CampaignG4/DeadSignal.vfs. Human G4 approval remains pending; these original procedural benchmarks are placeholders, not AAA art.

## Controls and tuning

Run PlayG4.cmd from the project folder. It launches a listen host with the reduced-memory test preset. WASD/mouse, E interaction and existing combat controls remain. Hold Left Shift while moving forward for a burst up to four seconds at 1.45 times armor walking speed. Release Shift and wait one second before restarting. Aiming, crouching, leaning, losing forward movement or becoming downed cancels sprint. A moving burst qualifies after the initial half-second threshold. Three qualifying bursts within the eight-second streak window trigger eight seconds of heavy breathing; additional qualifying bursts extend it. No health penalty or stamina percentage meter.

Press Left Ctrl or C to toggle crouch. F interacts (including revive, pickups, evidence and banking). Hold Q/E to peek left/right near blocking geometry. Free movement cover was positively reviewed by the user. While toggled crouched at low cover, Q or E temporarily stands the player over the top; release returns to crouch. An open edge on the chosen side selects a side lean instead and keeps the player crouched. Tall cover retains side leaning. Overhead collision prevents standing; leaving cover ends the peek. Move away to exit. Generic wall, parked vehicle hull and trunk collision can qualify; decorative foliage need not qualify. Cover gives no damage reduction: authoritative eye/muzzle traces determine obstruction. Eye offset is limited to 28 cm and shortened by a 9 cm sphere sweep. Crouched hit-region heights and body presentation are adjusted. Lean roll is presentation only; final animation/hand alignment is unfinished.

The server owns sprint timing, speed limits, the fatigue counter and geometry-validated lean. Intent travels through Unreal CharacterMovement saved/compressed moves, not client damage or teleport reports. Owning clients predict speed and receive the server clock; smooth correction/physical input feel still needs human review, especially under impairment. Guests remain session-only. Ordinary G4 campaigns use a separate save folder; opt-in diagnostic/capture fixtures use G1SaveRoot isolation.

## Real assets

ArtSource/G4 contains four editable .blend files and FBX exports: Carbine, Wall, Enemy_Static and Enemy_Rig. Blender 5.2.2 LTS actually ran the export. Manifest.json records bounds, triangles, UV layers and original-source provenance. Carbine: 1,040 triangles; wall: 432; enemy: 1,620. Enemy rig: 17 bones with rigid part weights, no animation clips or production deformation approval. Smart UVs are present; texel-density, unwrap quality and full art review remain pending.

Actual Unreal assets exist in Content/G4: three static meshes, skeletal benchmark, four simple PBR materials and a looping SoundWave. The importer asserts meter-to-centimeter dimensions (carbine 98 x 10 x 35.5 cm; wall 22 x 400 x 300 cm; enemy 31.5 x 143 x 181 cm). Existing validated 1 m cube and Blender -Y to Unreal +X pipeline are retained. Benchmarks sit east of HQ; vehicle and trunk shapes are collision proxies, not finished vehicle/tree art. Only the carbine has a new first-person/world mesh; other weapons remain greybox. The enemy skeletal asset is a benchmark, not integrated into squad animation.

HeavyBreathing.wav is original deterministic synthetic filtered noise, generated locally with no paid tool or human recording. One persistent AudioComponent per pawn starts/stops one loop, volume 0.25, attenuation radius 80 cm/falloff 320 cm. Human audibility, plausibility, spatial mix and final replacement approval are NOT RUN. Do not mistake the breathing status readout for sound-quality evidence.

## Repeatable commands

From the project folder in PowerShell:

    .\Scripts\G4.ps1 Build
    .\Scripts\G4.ps1 Import
    .\Scripts\G4.ps1 Test
    .\Scripts\G4.ps1 Package
    .\Scripts\G4.ps1 Host
    .\Scripts\G4.ps1 Join
    .\Scripts\G4.ps1 Editor
    .\Scripts\G4NetworkSmoke.ps1 -Rendered
    .\Scripts\G4NetworkSmoke.ps1 -RttMs 100 -LossPercent 2
    .\Scripts\G4NetworkSmoke.ps1 -RttMs 200 -LossPercent 5

G4.ps1 defaults to the audited installed engine path and validates the 5.8 line. Package refuses to overwrite the candidate; choose a new build ID in the scripts for a new archive. Import preserves existing mesh sources and recreates only the G4-labelled fixture actors in the G4 map. Editor tools are not shipped gameplay Python. FullQuality disables the reduced-memory rendering overrides.

Initial procedural-source creation command (refuses to overwrite existing .blend sources):

    & 'C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe' --background --python Scripts/Blender/export_g4.py

Export to a new revision filename; update the importer destination to a new asset ID to preserve prior review assets. Expected result: FBX beside editable source, then a genuine Unreal asset with matching centimeter bounds.
